## Pastbound v2.5.2

Pastbound now has ten additional relic bonus effects layered onto the existing ability system. Rosetta reveals nearby life and grants knowledge, Gilgamesh grants endurance, Anubis purges poison and wither while extinguishing fire, Minos gives a controlled lift, Roma produces aurei, Viking improves night navigation, Samurai reinforces absorption, Maya grants experience, Inka produces cordage, and Harappa produces clay. These are server-side, deterministic, and preserve the Turkish relic identifiers.

The time-machine emblem has been revised at high resolution with a stronger archaeological identity: hammered bronze, engraved astronomical scales, cuneiform, papyrus, astrolabe geometry, manuscript fragments, route charts, and lunar navigation details. The updated emblem is used by the mod metadata, the time-machine GUI, and the downloadable logo asset.

The touched relic code is organized around a named bonus-effect method and contains no newly added comment lines. Validation passed with ASSET_AUDIT_OK for 165 models and 96 textures, Python tool compilation, clean diff checks, and a successful compileJava Gradle task.
