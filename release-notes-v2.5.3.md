## Pastbound v2.5.3

This release corrects the logo direction: the supplied time-machine emblem remains the exact visual foundation, with improved material detail, edge clarity, archaeological artifact texture, and transparent presentation at 1920×1920. Compact 512×512 copies are used in Minecraft metadata and the time-machine GUI.

The ten additional relic bonuses are now expressed through named historical action methods instead of a dense anonymous switch. The effects remain server-side and preserve the Turkish identifier and event conventions.

A matching Pastbound-2.5.3.jar is attached. It contains 638 compiled class and resource entries, expanded NeoForge metadata with version 2.5.3, the logo asset, the custom-dimension advancement tree, and the relic implementation.
